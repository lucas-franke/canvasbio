{{organisation}} is committed to making this website accessible. It follows the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA and the European standard EN 301 549.

## Compliance status

This website is **partially compliant** with these requirements. The limitations are listed below.

## Non-accessible content

- **Easy-to-read German (Leichte Sprache):** information in Leichte Sprache is not offered at the moment.
- **Life stories:** the texts are automatically imported extracts from Wikipedia. Words and quotations in other languages are not marked up as such, so screen readers may pronounce them as English.
- **Image descriptions:** portraits have an alternative text with the person's name, but no detailed description of the picture.
- **Sign language:** information in German Sign Language is not offered at the moment.

The mosaic can be used with the keyboard and with buttons, without swiping. For screen readers, the **list view** is the equivalent alternative with the same content.

## Preparation of this statement

This statement was prepared on {{statementDate}}. It is based on a self-assessment using automated checks (axe-core on all pages and states, in light and dark mode, on desktop, Android and iOS) and manual checks of keyboard use, zoom and contrast.

## Feedback and contact

Have you found a barrier, or do you need content in a different format? Please write to us: [{{contactEmail}}](mailto:{{contactEmail}}). We usually reply within two weeks.

## Enforcement procedure

If you do not receive a satisfactory response, you can request a conciliation procedure with the conciliation body under § 16 of the German Disability Equality Act (BGG), at the Federal Government Commissioner for Matters relating to Persons with Disabilities. The procedure is free of charge and you do not need legal representation. More information (in German): [www.schlichtungsstelle-bgg.de](https://www.schlichtungsstelle-bgg.de/).
