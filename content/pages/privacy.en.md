## In short

This website sets no cookies, uses no analytics or tracking tools and embeds no third-party content. Fonts and images are loaded from the same server as the page.

## Server log files

When you visit the website, the server stores technically necessary data in log files: IP address, date and time, requested address, amount of data transferred, browser and operating system. They are used for secure operation and troubleshooting (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) and are deleted after a short period.

## Storage in your browser

So that the site remembers your settings, it stores a few items in your browser's storage (localStorage and sessionStorage):

- your chosen appearance (light, dark or as on your device),
- which biographies you have opened, so that "Random biography" prefers new ones,
- your position in the mosaic during a visit.

This information stays on your device and is not sent to us. It is required for the features you use (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG). You can delete it at any time in your browser settings.

## Links to other websites

Sources such as Wikipedia are linked, not embedded. Only when you follow a link do the privacy terms of that website apply.

## Contact by e-mail

If you write to us, we process your details to answer your request (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR) and delete them once they are no longer needed for that.

## Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure and restriction of processing of your data, to data portability and to object to processing. You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. You can reach us at [{{contactEmail}}](mailto:{{contactEmail}}).
