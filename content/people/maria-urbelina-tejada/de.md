María Urbelina Tejada was an Argentine politician. She was elected to the Chamber of Deputies in 1951 as one of the first group of female parliamentarians in Argentina.

## Biography

In the 1951 legislative elections she was a Peronist Party candidate in San Juan and was one of the 26 women elected to the Chamber of Deputies. She remained in office until 1955.
